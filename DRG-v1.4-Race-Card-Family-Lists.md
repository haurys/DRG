# v1.4 Race Card Family Lists

## What this document is

A complete audit of the 108 physical Race cards, including Conditions.

## How to use it

Read the family tables for printed values. The change ledger identifies every v1.4 title edit; the effect text is copied from the current card data.

## Key takeaways

There are 20 Training, 20 Gear, 22 Fuel, 30 Event, and 16 Condition cards. Fourteen physical cards receive title-only changes; Nausea receives a wording-only clarification and three Gear categories are corrected. No printed Effect, Effort, Energy, or Severity changes in this candidate.
