# Superseded Race Card mechanics

This is a historical boundary document. The [2026-09-30 canonical rules](DRG-Canonical-Playtest-Rules.md), `RaceCards.docx` frozen table, and explicit designer rulings govern current play. Archived Run-01 through Run-05 audit files retain their old mechanics as evidence and are unchanged.

| Earlier behavior | Frozen redesign |
|---|---|
| Event Effort and mandatory Effect together; Perfect Rhythm without Effect | Every non-Condition card has exclusive MOVEMENT or EFFECT use. Perfect Rhythm's EFFECT gives +2 direct Movement. |
| No card Energy cost, or Effort as Energy spent | Per MOVEMENT card: Effort 1–3 costs 0 Energy, 4–6 costs 1, 7+ costs 2; EFFECT use pays no card cost. |
| Training/Gear directly add Effort; Pace Watch Pace Effort | Installed Energy/Difficulty/Gut/Condition effects only; Pace Watch gains +1 Energy on each of two runner-turns. |
| Fuel +1 Energy; Nausea reduced Fuel recovery | Fuel +2 Energy or its named remedy/Severity branch; active Nausea bars Fuel Effect use. |
| Nausea printed `No Fuel Allowed` | Printed `Cannot use Fuel` now means no Fuel **Effect** while active; legal Fuel Movement remains possible. This is a wording clarification, not a mechanical change. |
| Stomach Trouble / old second Dehydrated | Gashed Knee / Cold Chills. |
| Second Hydration Belt / EV-010 Congestion | Weather Shell / Cold Snap. |
| Wrong Playlist | EV-030 Untied Lace. |
| Helpful Runner transferred its own card and awarded Energy to the giver | The frozen Effect awards +1 Energy to another Runner; no card transfer. |
| Generic Effort treatment spent a Training/Gear card's printed Effort to reduce any Condition | Each card has only mutually exclusive Movement or printed Effect use; Condition reductions occur through named Effects and remedies. |
| Hot Spell imposed an extra Push Pace cost globally | The frozen Event stages a one-round 1-Energy loss only for its player; Cold Snap uses the same Cold timing. |
| Hydration Belt gained 1 Energy every runner-turn | It gains 1 at an applicable Water opportunity. |
| Mental Toughness +2 and Pace Band +1 Gut Check | Mental Toughness +3 and Pace Band +2. |
| Condition penalty scaled by Effective Severity | Printed binary penalties while Effective Severity > 0; treatment and suppression still use Severity. |
| Porta-Potty fixed half-mile cap | Its printed EFFECT is −1 direct Movement and +2 Energy after Movement; ordinary two-mile cap remains. |

The current Pace, Course, Pack, Will, hand, draw, Finish, and milestone rules are in the canonical document. The former Pace-only Will fallback is superseded: Will now covers exact combined Pace and Movement-card Energy shortfall after reductions.

Any earlier description treating a whole Course card as a one-mile segment is superseded. A physical Course card contributes two total miles in opposing one-mile lanes; the data keeps one record per lane. The marathon assembles 13 cards from Start/Finish and returns on their opposite lanes.

## v1.3 supersessions

Choosing only one Aid-eligible or Water-eligible Condition is superseded: each eligible active Condition resolves. An in-race assistance swap uses the requester's Treat/Prepare action but no card-play slot, with no helper action debt.

## v1.4 candidate supersessions

Seven-card starts and up-to-two-card replenishment are superseded by six-card starts and one normal draw below the six-card maximum. The rule that an installed Training/Gear card cannot supply Effort is superseded by Direct Slot Movement after two complete runner turns. Returning attached Gear to hand or a normal slot when its Condition leaves is superseded: the card discards. The earlier RR-11 relocation policy remains historical for equipped Gear; an attached treatment card cannot be reassigned. The former 3–20 intended base-game range is superseded by 4–12, preferably 6–8. All four Pace values and fixed stations remain unchanged.
