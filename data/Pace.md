# Shared Pace card — Stage 2R candidate

| Pace | Effort bonus | Base Energy cost |
|---|---:|---:|
| Steady | +1 | 1 |
| Race | +2 | 2 |
| Push | +3 | 3 |

Choose at Round Start. Pay once per runner-turn after legal preservation, separately from Movement-card costs. A stopped runner removes the Pace token and cannot receive Pack preservation. A resumed runner chooses a payable legal Pace; Pack membership is not granted retroactively.
