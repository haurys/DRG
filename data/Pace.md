# Pace

| Pace | Movement modifier | Energy cost | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Easy | 0 | 0 | PLAYTEST ASSUMPTION |
| Steady | 1 | -1 | PLAYTEST ASSUMPTION |
| Race | 2 | -1 | PLAYTEST ASSUMPTION |
| Push | 3 | -2 | PLAYTEST ASSUMPTION |

Initial selection is open; all tokens lock before race start. No runner changes Pace on Turn 1. Redeclaration occurs at the start of Turn 2+.
