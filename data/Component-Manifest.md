# Component Manifest — v1.4 candidate

| Component | Count / shape | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Race cards | 108; Training 20, Gear 20, Fuel 22, Event 30, Condition 16 | Frozen; physical IDs unique |
| Course cards | 30 selectable physical cards, 2 total miles and two one-mile lanes per card; 60 one-mile data sides | Marathon connects 13 physical cards from Start/Finish for 13 miles out and 13 back |
| Shared Pace card | 1, Easy ▷ / Steady ▶ / Race ▶▶ / Push ▶▶▶ | Unchanged |
| Runner tokens | 1 per runner | Logical component |
| Will tokens | 1 per runner | Single-use exact Pace/Movement Energy shortfall payment |
| Finish extension | Marathon 0.2 / half 0.1 mile | Unchanged |

Movement is `→`, Energy `⚡`. Printed Movement Energy costs are 0 for Effort 1–3, ⚡ for 4–6, and ⚡⚡ for 7+. Conditions print Severity and no Movement cost.

The six-card opening hand and 4–12 supported players (preferably 6–8) use the same 108-card deck. Normal slots and Condition attachments remain out of the discard reshuffle.

Each physical Course card has four quarter-mile arrows/spaces on each one-mile lane; the Start/Finish card anchors one end of the visible out-and-back marathon row. Data stores each lane separately. Station and Gut Check mileage remain fixed regardless of selected Course cards.
