# DRG Events — current 30-card playtest inventory

FROZEN: Events only play during **Movement**, at most **one per runner-turn**, counting toward the two-card play limit. Each played Event contributes its printed Effort **and** its mandatory effect when one exists. No Event activates on draw, while held, or when discarded unplayed. After-Movement Energy cannot fund a Pace cost already paid. Staged/Course/Global cards remain in the applicable state until resolved/expired and are then discarded. `OTHER` Target Lock blocks a second staged/active OTHER Event on the same opponent; other scopes and non-OTHER beneficial interactions do not inherit that lock. +1/−1 Movement means +/−0.25 mile **after** base Effort conversion, before shared Course traversal. Effect Energy is not automatically the card's separate printed Energy; its value may remain unknown.

## Execution and review notes

- OTHER effects choose one eligible opponent when played. If intended target is locked, select another eligible opponent or make a different legal play. Once a staged OTHER Event resolves/expires, lock ends. Headwind, Congestion, Potholes, Wild Goose Chase, Untied Lace use OTHER. High Five and Helpful Runner are beneficial interactions, not automatically OTHER.
- Tough Decision cycles zero, one, or two eligible other cards. Each cycled card requires one normal replacement; newly revealed Conditions activate immediately and do not alter completed Movement.
- Porta-Potty's after-Movement +2 cannot pay current Pace. Its older `Stop; +2 Energy` text is SUPERSEDED.
- Perfect Rhythm's old Effort 8/+2 Pace Effort and proposed Pace preservation are SUPERSEDED. EV-017 Pace Buddy, EV-025 Goose Chase, and EV-030 Wrong Playlist are SUPERSEDED at those slots.
- Sudden Rain tracks each copy independently. High Five is not OTHER-targeted. Helpful Runner may circulate repeatedly under the same transfer and replay-delay rules.
