# Virtual Runners

| ID | Profile | Policy |
|---|---|---|
| P1 | Balanced | Balances Effort, Energy, and remaining distance. |
| P2 | Aggressive | Prefers Race/Push and high Effort while Energy permits. |
| P3 | Energy Conservative | Prefers Easy/Steady and recovery cards at low Energy. |
| P4 | Course Planner | Reserves high Effort for difficult current/upcoming terrain. |
| P5 | Pack Runner | Prefers matching the nearest runner pace. |
| P6 | Opportunist | Prefers immediately useful recovery and strong Effort combinations. |
| P7 | Front Runner | Prefers the fastest sustainable pace while leading or close to lead. |
| P8 | Adaptive | Weights Energy, position, difficulty, and distance remaining dynamically. |

All profiles follow identical game rules and differ only in transparent scoring weights. They may inspect public course, milestones, positions, paces, Conditions and Effects, plus their own hand. They never inspect opponents’ hands.
