# Simulation Decision Rules — Victory Directive

**Top-level AI objective:** finish before all opponents in the fewest practical turns. This directive governs every profile and takes precedence over resource conservation, installation preference, and local card heuristics. Rules and card data remain authoritative. A profile can change a small tactical tie preference; it cannot justify losing a legal positive-Movement turn solely to conserve resources or install a card.

## Decision timing and information

- At Round Start, compare each legal Pace with the best complete plan available from the runner's current hand. Pace locks before Replenish. The new draws are unknown and are not previewed. Condition Pace caps are filtered before scoring. Pack membership is decided after Pace selection; Pace previews do not reuse the previous round's Pack.
- After Replenish, enumerate complete legal turn plans from the now-known hand: no Treat/Prepare, Training installation/replacement, Gear equip/replacement/attachment, and Fuel Energy or named-remedy choice; combine each with zero, one, or two legal Movement cards within the two-card budget. A card used for its Effect contributes no printed Effort. The one-Event limit, OTHER target eligibility, slots, footwear, and Fuel restrictions are validated before scoring. A plan with no Movement card is considered only if no legal card remains.
- Score each plan from its projected post-Treat/Prepare state and projected Pace payment. Use the actual Movement Model A conversion, installed Energy triggers and their timing, paid per-card Movement Energy, Effective Difficulty at visible Course boundaries, Direct Movement, current staged modifiers, and the Finish extension. The preview uses no future draws or random outcomes. Event Effects execute only when the Event is played in EFFECT mode.
- The scoring priority is immediate forward progress and Finish, with visible opponent position increasing the value of progress when trailing. Estimated turns remaining uses visible distance and projected Movement as a heuristic. Ending Energy, Will, retained hand Effort, current-condition relief, and installed effects support future progress. Installations account for the removed card, the consumed Movement play, the replaced installed effect, and a future payoff discounted by remaining visible distance. A zero-Movement plan is excluded when a positive-Movement plan exists unless Energy is at most 1 or an active Condition has Effective Severity at least 4; even then it must win the full score comparison.
- Profile modifiers are bounded secondary preferences. Aggressive and Front Runner slightly favor a faster legal Pace at equal progress; Energy Conservative values a small reserve; Pack Runner uses nearby runners' publicly known previous Pace as a small Pack opportunity estimate; Course Planner and Opportunist receive limited tactical tie preference. Balanced and Adaptive use the shared public-state score. All profiles apply the same progress, Finish, and legality precedence.
- OTHER Event Effects target the furthest advanced unlocked eligible opponent, with stable player ID ties. Pre-race Deck Exchanges assess the surrendered card primarily by printed Effort with Energy recovery as a tie-break. The AI exchanges only below the public-deck expected value; replacement identity is never inspected before exchange. Acquired cards stay locked.

The complete selected turn plan is logged as `AI_TURN_PLAN` after Replenish. The later `TREAT_PREPARE`, `PACE_PAYMENT`, `PLAY`, `MOVE`, and Event emissions remain authoritative for executed effects. Preview scores are deterministic estimates, not game rules or guarantees of future draws.

## Decision rationale observability

Each unscripted AI runner-turn also emits `AI_TURN_RATIONALE` after Replenish and before executing the selected plan, followed by `AI_TURN_OUTCOME` after Movement. These records are observational; turning rationale logging off leaves the selected plan and game state unchanged. `Simulation-Diagnostics.json` includes an `ai_rationale` summary with zero-Movement and setup opportunity-cost counts. Scripted fixture turns do not represent AI decisions and emit no AI rationale.

The rationale includes the public race position and gaps, current visible Course and installed state, the Round Start Pace options and preservation cost, counts and best examples for every legal complete-plan class, the selected plan, rejected alternatives, every rejected plan that projected more Movement, and the strongest rejected zero-Movement plan. Redundant card permutations are summarized by class counts. A plan detail includes card roles, projected Effort, Movement, Pace Energy, Condition changes, setup opportunity cost, visible future-effect estimates and remaining-distance discount, the existing total score, and estimated turns remaining. The pre-plan turns estimate applies the scorer's projected rate to the current remaining distance; it is a diagnostic comparison, not another selection rule. Some score components and after-Event Energy are not independently exposed by the existing scorer and are labeled accordingly.

Primary reason codes are `WIN_SPEED`, `FINISH_SPRINT`, `POSITION_RECOVERY`, `ENERGY_SUSTAINABILITY`, `CONDITION_EMERGENCY`, `SETUP_PAYOFF`, `PACK_ADVANTAGE`, `LEGALITY_FORCED`, and `NO_POSITIVE_MOVEMENT_AVAILABLE`. Secondary position, Pack, and finish-phase reasons may accompany the primary code. Codes describe an existing choice; they do not cause it. The outcome record distinguishes a projected zero from an actual zero and identifies whether a legal positive-Movement alternative existed, an emergency comparison was allowed, or no positive plan was available. Actual Movement and card effects in the live events remain authoritative.

## Playtest procedures

RR-13 Pack grouping remains the existing deterministic PLAYTEST ASSUMPTION: virtual runners consent to the nearest legal same-Pace group within 0.5 mile at Round Start. Potholes chooses Energy loss when available and Movement loss at zero Energy. RR-11, RR-16, and RR-17 remain unresolved where designated in the canonical rules. No marathon is executed as part of this AI implementation pass.

## Frozen card economy

The AI compares each complete legal plan after Pace payment and every selected MOVEMENT card’s 0/1/2 Energy cost. EVENT EFFECT mode adds no printed Effort or card cost. Fuel restoration is +2 (or the chosen remedy/Severity branch), and installed Training/Gear now typically gain or preserve Energy instead of adding Movement Effort. Visible terrain/Pace triggers, short equipment durations, replacement opportunity cost, and remaining-distance discount enter the existing Victory score. Future draws and future opponent choices remain hidden. Gut Check modifiers are Strength +1, Mental Toughness +3, Pace Band +2.

Training Condition reductions are evaluated on installation and acquisition. Hydration Belt is valued at visible future Water opportunities, not as an unconditional runner-turn gain. Weather protection concerns a staged self Energy loss, independent of Pace preservation. Helpful Runner grants Energy to another Runner without circulating its physical card.
