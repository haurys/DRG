# v1.4 Course Interaction Matrix

## What this document is

A coverage audit joining the 60 one-mile lanes of 30 physical two-mile Course cards to current Race-card counterplay.

## How to use it

Read frequency and side IDs first, then support and strategy. “General” means Pace, affordable Effort, Fuel, Will, and visible planning; it is not a new specialist Effect.

## Key takeaways

Trail routes and inclines support some difficult natural lanes; Dirt, Grass, Rolling and uneven footing still warrant measurement. Rare Brick, Cobblestone, and Sand retain general counterplay. No mechanical card Effect changes are justified by the inventory alone.

## How coverage is judged

**WELL COVERED** has multiple practical direct or general choices; **PARTIALLY COVERED** has limited direct support; **INTENTIONALLY UNSUPPORTED** has no property-specific Effect but can use general movement; **POTENTIAL COVERAGE GAP** calls for observation, not automatic rebalancing. Counts refer to all 60 one-mile data lanes; a marathon selects 13 physical cards and traverses 26 lanes, one outbound and one return per card.

## Physical mapping and Option C re-evaluation

Each physical card contributes one one-mile outbound lane **and** its opposite one-mile return lane if selected for the 13-card marathon. All 60 side IDs and frequencies above are inventory frequencies; one selected physical card binds its two sides together. A specialist can be installed in anticipation of a visible lane, retained through it, then sacrificed only after two complete runner turns, with its Effect ending before that Movement. The fixed Start/Finish and stations do not add surface bonuses.

Thematically, broadening existing Trail Runs to natural surfaces or Form Drills to uneven footing could create useful coverage, but those are **unapproved alternatives**, not rules. The inventory shows frequency and possible overlap, not an observed Movement deficit or the size of any needed benefit. Changing printed Effects now would confound comparison with v1.3.2 and could create a redundant always-useful card. **No mechanical card Effect, printed cost, card count, or Course value changes in this remediation.** The later authorized multi-seed evaluation should measure progress and slot retention at these mapped lanes before any targeted balance decision.
