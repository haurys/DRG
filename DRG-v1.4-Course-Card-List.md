# v1.4 Course Card List

## What this document is

A complete inventory of **30 physical two-mile Course cards**, each with two **one-mile lane/data records** (60 records total).

## How to use it

Read each row as one physical card with its A and B lanes together. The selected physical card can be oriented either way; its assigned race-mile positions depend on its place in the visible connected row. Difficulty and surface are properties of a lane, not an extra card.

## Key takeaways

A marathon joins 13 two-mile physical cards long-side to long-side from Start/Finish: one lane is traversed outward, and the opposite lane returns toward Finish. Each one-mile lane has four quarter-mile arrows/spaces. The 60 lane records have printed Difficulty 1–8 (mean 3.5). No Course values or station mileage changed.

## Physical layout and simulation representation

The separate Start/Finish card anchors one end of the 13-card row. Runners start there, run miles 1–13 on one edge, turn at the far end, then run miles 14–26 on the opposite edges of the same cards in reverse physical order, finishing at Start/Finish. Position 1 therefore supplies miles 1 and 26; position 13 supplies miles 13 and 14. Each selected card contributes **two total miles**. The simulator stores a selected lane as a separate **one-mile side record** and assigns it a race-mile number. A 13-mile half uses seven physical cards with one return lane unused. All Course information is visible before exchanges, enabling Pace, Training, Gear, treatment, station approach, slot retention and sacrifice planning. No separately printed card title field exists; setting + route below is a display description, not a data edit.

Water, Aid, and Gut Checks attach to **race mileage**, not to any permanently fixed physical card. No source lane has a special rule or extra restriction.
