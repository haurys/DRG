# v1.4 Annotated Example Race

## What this document is

A guided four-runner illustration of decisions across consecutive turns, with short later-race scenes. It is **not** a seeded balance simulation or an audit result.

## How to use it

Read the opening sequence in order; later-mile scenes demonstrate milestone and recovery rules without claiming a complete generated race trace.

## Key takeaways

Installing a card trades present hand liquidity for an immediate Effect and delayed Effort; assistance and Will can rescue a runner, but neither creates an extra turn action.

## Set the table

Ana, Bo, Cy, and Dee choose a marathon. Connect 13 physical two-mile cards long-side to long-side from the separate Start/Finish card. Each has one one-mile outbound lane and an opposite one-mile return lane, each divided into four quarter-mile spaces. Runners begin at Start, run away along one edge, turn at the far end, and return toward Start/Finish along the other. Shuffle, select, orient and reveal all 13 connected cards, including its side Difficulties, surfaces, inclines, and fixed Water miles 4/8/12/16/20/24, Aid miles 8/16/24, and Gut Checks 10/18/23. The Course itself has no printed specialist names. Each runner receives six normal cards. Suppose Ana has Pace Practice (TR-013), Road Trainers (GE-001), Energy Gel (FU-001), a legal Event, and two other normal cards. These are **illustrative hands**, not an asserted random draw. If a Condition appears during setup, place it in the runner’s Condition area and continue seeking its replacement normal card. With the route visible, Ana may spend one of three pre-race exchange moves to seek a useful card; this example keeps her hand.

## Consecutive runner turns: investment and release

| Turn | Choice | Why and what happens |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | All four choose legal Paces simultaneously; Ana selects Steady. No Pack forms in round 1. She installs Pace Practice with Treat/Prepare, then uses one hand card for Movement. | Installation spends the one Treat/Prepare opportunity **and card play 1**, leaving Ana only **one** normal card play for Movement this turn. It starts its Effect now. Replenish occurred first and draws nothing if still at six. Steady's base Pace cost is 1 Energy; Pace Practice preserves that 1 Energy when installed, so Ana pays 0 Pace Energy. There is no Pack preservation in round 1. The Training stays in its slot after the turn. |
| 2 | Ana Replenishes one normal card if below six. She keeps Pace Practice installed, optionally equips Road Trainers using the one Treat/Prepare **and one card play**, and moves with at most one other card. | The Training is only one runner turn old and cannot be liquidated. Gear installation begins its own independent clock. Road Trainers has an Asphalt-related printed Effect; do not treat its Effort as available merely because a later Asphalt section is visible. |
| 3 | Ana may use Pace Practice directly for Movement, perhaps with one hand card. | Installed on turn 1, it survived turns 1 and 2; it is first eligible now. Remove its Steady/Condition Effect **before** choosing the payable Movement result. The printed Effort 6 costs one Movement Energy and consumes one of two card plays; discard after use. Gear installed on turn 2 is not yet eligible. |
| 4 | If Road Trainers remained equipped through turns 2 and 3, Ana may now sacrifice it directly, with another eligible slot or hand card if payable. | Its Asphalt Effect ends first. Printed Effort 7 costs two Energy; Will may cover an exact legal shortfall. A new card installed as a replacement this turn starts a new clock; it cannot be moved immediately. |

The scene does not assert a particular distance or cost after all Course modifiers: the actual selected side, Conditions and card combinations determine that calculation. A runner with zero cards in hand still attempts the one normal draw below six; an eligible slot can supply Movement without returning to hand.

## Later-mile scenes on the same visible route

**Condition and attachment:** Cy draws a Condition and places it immediately. If it is Cramp and Cy has compatible Compression Sleeves, Cy can spend Treat/Prepare attaching that Gear to this specific Condition. It leaves hand and costs Treat/Prepare **and one card play**; only one Movement play remains. It cannot be a slot or assistance card, and its printed Effort is unavailable. A partial treatment keeps the attachment committed. On complete removal by legal remedy, the attached Sleeves discard. A persistent Condition at Effective Severity zero is not necessarily removed.

**Fuel and Nausea:** Bo uses Energy Gel through Treat/Prepare **and one card play** for +2 Energy, gaining no printed Effort. Nausea prints `Cannot use Fuel`: if active, Bo cannot use the Fuel **Effect**. Energy Gel remains a legal Effort-3 Movement card costing zero Energy if other Movement checks pass.

**Event and Pack:** Dee plays Crowd Support Effect while another runner is active; its target must be eligible and the Event contributes no printed Effort. If all others have finished or DNF, its Effect is illegal but its Movement use remains possible. Pack membership remains the Round Start snapshot even if runners separate during this round; next round may form a different Pack.

**Stations and Gut Check:** Reaching Water mile 8 removes all active Dehydrated Conditions (and attached cards on complete removal); Aid at the same mile treats **every** eligible active Condition, including Nausea and Gashed Knee, and applies printed reductions to Twisted Ankle and Heat Exhaustion. Resolve station Energy separately. At mile 10 Gut Check threshold 25, count only printed Effort in cards still in hand plus applicable Strength/Mental Toughness/Pace Band bonus; neither installed nor attached cards count. Failure costs 2 Energy without discarding the counted cards.

**Stranding, assistance and Will:** A runner at zero Energy with Will spent is not automatically DNF. Check free Movement in hand, an eligible installed card, Fuel/recovery that is actually legal, future draw, and reachable stations. If stranded, the runner may spend the one Treat/Prepare requesting a one-for-one swap with any eligible active runner. A received Movement card may be used now within the remaining two-card budget; a received card requiring Treat/Prepare may support recovery next turn, but cannot receive a second Treat/Prepare now. The helper incurs no future debt. If the runner still has Will instead, it may pay the exact shortfall of an otherwise-legal turn. Only after all practical paths fail can DNF be recorded.

**The finish:** Ana traverses the opposite lanes back toward the Start/Finish end. The marathon Course stops at 26.0 miles; Ana standing exactly there needs a positive legal quarter-mile Movement unit to cross the extra 0.2-mile extension. Crossing ends her Movement immediately. At Round End, any same-round finishers compare printed Effort remaining in hand, then Energy, then unused Will, sharing place if still tied. The half marathon similarly extends 13.0 by 0.1. A runner may finish on zero Energy.
