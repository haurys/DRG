# Race Formats

## Marathon

13 Course cards form one row. Outbound position `n` is mile `n`; return position `n` is mile `27-n`. This gives 26 miles / 104 quarter-mile spaces. Separate Finish: 0.2 mile.

## Half marathon

7 cards. Outbound positions 1–7 are miles 1–7. Return positions 6–1 are miles 8–13; side B of card 7 is unused. This gives 13 miles / 52 quarter-mile spaces. Separate Finish: 0.1 mile.

Course order and orientation are randomized and visible.

At either Course endpoint, arriving with no positive legal Movement remaining does not finish. One further legal quarter-mile Movement unit crosses the respective 0.2/0.1-mile extension, which has no Difficulty.
