# Race Formats

## Marathon

Connect 13 physical two-mile Course cards long-side to long-side from the Start/Finish card at one end. Each card has a one-mile outbound lane and a one-mile return lane, each divided into four quarter-mile spaces. Runners start at Start, travel away along one side, turn, then return along the other side to the Start/Finish end. Outbound position `n` is mile `n`; return position `n` is mile `27-n`. This gives 26 miles / 104 quarter-mile spaces. Separate Finish: 0.2 mile.

## Half marathon

7 physical two-mile cards. Outbound positions 1–7 are miles 1–7. Return positions 6–1 are miles 8–13; the opposite lane of card 7 is unused. This gives 13 miles / 52 quarter-mile spaces. Separate Finish: 0.1 mile.

Course order and orientation are randomized and visible.

The 30-card selectable inventory is represented as 60 one-mile **side records** in structured data. A side record is one lane of a two-mile physical card, never a whole physical card. Visible lanes and fixed station positions support Pace, Training/Gear, treatment, station approach, slot retention, and slot sacrifice planning.

At either Course endpoint, arriving with no positive legal Movement remaining does not finish. One further legal quarter-mile Movement unit crosses the respective 0.2/0.1-mile extension, which has no Difficulty.
